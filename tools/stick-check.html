Stick Check

Checks that your radio is readable as a USB joystick, and shows which axis each stick drives.

  1. Plug in the RadioMaster Pocket and choose USB Joystick (HID) on the radio.
  2. Open this page in Chrome and click on it once.
  3. Move a stick or flip a switch. Chrome only shows a controller after it has been used.
